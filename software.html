MeatOnBone Plugin

Have you ever wondered what it means to "add meat to your mix"? Well I was wondering that to and came to the conlcusion that a careful balance of compression and distortion was the answer. MeatOnBone allows you to apply this treatment to one of 4 frequency bands [aptly labelled: 'Beef', 'Pork', 'Chicken', and 'Fish'] and mix in in parallel. Apologies if you are vegan, I'll make a vegetable based plugin next! ._.

Educational Bridge Builder [VR APP]

CamBRIDGE Builder! is a splendid VR bridge-building simulation tool that combines all the fun and insight from classical spaghetti bridge building experiments with scientific accuracy computations to create an unrivaled learning experience for everyone, from middle schoolers to civil engineers and seniors. It aims to (1) spark interest in bridge design by making the building and simulating experience fun; (2) provide an educational platform for design analysis, thus reinforcing intuition about existing designs. The app combines the best of experiences familiar to mechanical and civil engineering students: the accuracy of pencil and paper calculations, the fun of building actual bridges out of spaghetti and the insight gained from 3D design simulators.
